Order of Precedence (Evaluation Order)
When combining multiple logical operators without parentheses, expressions are evaluated from highest precedence to lowest.
Priority Operator Name Symbol Rule / Note
1 Parentheses ( ) Always evaluated first; overrides default precedence order.
2 Negation (NOT) ¬, ~ Highest operator precedence; applies directly to the target statement.
3 Conjunction (AND) ∧, & Evaluated before OR, XOR, Conditional, or Biconditional operators.
4 Disjunction (OR) & XOR ∨, ⊕ Evaluated after AND, but before implication operators.
5 Conditional (IF...THEN) → Evaluated after standard logical connectives.
6 Biconditional (IFF) ↔ Lowest operator precedence; evaluated last.
